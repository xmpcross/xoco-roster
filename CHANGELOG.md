# Changelog

All notable changes to Roster are documented here.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-21

- Updated Astro to 7.4.1 and other dependencies.
- Updated `AGENTS.md` for Astro 7.
- Files check out with LF line endings on every platform.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-08

- Initial commercial release.
- Astro 7 theme for recruitment and executive search firms with home, jobs, job, sectors, sector, employers, submit a vacancy, candidates, register, salary guide, placements, placement, consultants, consultant, insights, topic archive, article, search, about, contact, privacy, RSS, robots, sitemap, and 404 routes.
- Jobs board with keyword search, faceted filters with live counts, sorting, and filters in the URL.
- Job pages with key facts, the consultant running the search, an apply form with CV upload, and JobPosting structured data.
- Multi-step vacancy form with validation and a review step, and candidate registration with CV upload.
- Forms with a demo mode until an endpoint is set.
- Salary guide with city tables, a salary calculator, and a report download form.
- Sector pages, consultant profiles, and placement case studies as content collections.
- Consultation booking with a Cal.com or Calendly embed or a built-in slot picker.
- Header that hides on scroll down, with full-width mega menus fed by sectors, roles, placements, and the team, and a full-screen mobile menu.
- Homepage with product-style panels of a search, a shortlist, a candidate's matches, and a salary check.
- Field of candidate portraits that pop in and out across a dot grid.
- Grainy colour aurora behind the hero search and the closing call to action.
- Insights with topics, a lead article, a table of contents, a reading-progress bar, a share sheet, and search.
- Photography from Pexels with credits.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap, RSS, Open Graph, Twitter/X cards, and JSON-LD.
- Self-hosted Host Grotesk, Lucide icons, and colour and type tokens in one stylesheet.
