## The role

The group runs 14 independent hospitals and four diagnostic centres across London and the South East, with annual revenue of £520m. Around 40% of its activity is NHS-funded elective surgery, and demand has risen faster than theatre capacity. The Clinical Operations Director left in September, and the group is running a permanent search alongside this interim appointment. You will report to the Chief Operating Officer and work closely with the Group Medical Director and the Chief Nurse.

## What you will do

- Lead the 14 hospital directors and the central operations team, who together manage around 3,200 staff.
- Raise theatre utilisation from 71% towards the 85% target in the annual plan.
- Improve the flow of NHS referrals, including waiting times for first outpatient appointments.
- Prepare two hospitals for CQC inspections expected early next year.
- Hand over a clear plan and set of measures to the permanent hire.

## What you will bring

- Senior operational leadership in acute care, ideally in both NHS and independent sector settings.
- A record of improving theatre productivity and elective activity.
- Experience of preparing hospitals for CQC inspection.
- The ability to lead a dispersed network and spend time on site at each hospital.
- Availability to start within four weeks.

## The package

A day rate of £900 to £1,100 outside IR35 for six months, five days a week. Based at the London support office, with regular travel across the group. Interviews with the COO take place in the week of 19 October.
