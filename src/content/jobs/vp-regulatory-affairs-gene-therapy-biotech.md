## The role

The company develops AAV gene therapies for inherited metabolic disorders, with its lead programme in a pivotal Phase 3 trial and two further candidates in Phase 1. Topline data are expected in the second half of next year, followed by a Biologics License Application under Breakthrough Therapy designation. Regulatory work has so far been led by the Chief Medical Officer with support from consultants, and the company now needs a full-time head of the function. You will report to the CMO and join a senior leadership team of 12.

## What you will do

- Own global regulatory strategy for the portfolio, starting with the BLA and a parallel marketing authorisation application to the EMA.
- Lead all interactions with the FDA's Office of Therapeutic Products, including the pre-BLA meeting.
- Build a team of four over the next year across regulatory strategy, CMC and operations.
- Work with manufacturing and quality on the CMC sections, where gene therapy filings most often stall.
- Advise on paediatric plans, expedited pathways and labelling.

## What you will bring

- At least 12 years in regulatory affairs, including direct leadership of a BLA or NDA through to approval.
- Experience with cell or gene therapy, or another complex biologic.
- A record of leading FDA meetings yourself, not supporting them.
- Working knowledge of EMA procedures, including PRIME.
- The ability to explain regulatory risk clearly to the board and to investors.

## The package

Base of $290,000 to $330,000, a 30% target bonus, an equity grant and full benefits. Three days a week in the Kendall Square office. The process includes a meeting with the CEO and a presentation to the leadership team, and should complete within five weeks.
