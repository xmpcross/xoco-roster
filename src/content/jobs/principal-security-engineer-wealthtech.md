## The role

The platform provides investment accounts and pensions to 400,000 retail customers and holds around £9bn in client assets. It is regulated by the FCA, runs entirely on AWS and deploys to production around 60 times a day. The security team of five has so far focused on governance and audit, and what it lacks is a senior engineer who can harden the platform from inside the codebase. You will report to the Head of Security and work alongside the platform and SRE teams.

## What you will do

- Set the technical direction for cloud, application and identity security across 140 engineers.
- Lead threat modelling on new products, starting with a self-invested personal pension launching next year.
- Build guardrails into the CI/CD pipeline and Terraform modules so that secure defaults need no manual review.
- Lead the response to serious incidents and run quarterly exercises with engineering leads.
- Mentor two security engineers and help define the career path above senior level.

## What you will bring

- Deep hands-on experience securing AWS, with strong knowledge of IAM, KMS and network controls.
- Production experience with Kubernetes and infrastructure as code.
- The ability to read and write code, ideally in Go, Python or TypeScript.
- Time in a regulated environment, with an understanding of what the FCA expects on operational resilience.
- A practical approach. You would rather remove a risk with a well-designed control than a policy document.

## The package

Base of £130,000 to £150,000, a 10% bonus, share options, an 8% pension and a £2,000 annual learning budget. Two days a week in the office near Old Street. The process runs to three stages over three weeks.
